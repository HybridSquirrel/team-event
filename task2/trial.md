The protagonist reaches the warehouse.
As you approach the heavy wooden door reinforced with iron bands. There is no handle, only a strange circular lock embedded in the center.

At roughly head height, the door has a small peephole punched through it.
As you near the door they hear multiple voices from the other side:
> "Seventeen... Seventeen... Seventeen..."
The chant repeats endlessly.

You inspect the door discovering that it is firmly locked. There appears to be a glowing hand doing rock, paper, scissor motion within the strange circular lock.

You decide to look though the peephole, you only see dakrness. Then-
> POKE!
A finger sharply jab you in the eye. You recoil out of pain.

From behind the door comes a burst of cheering and applause.
The chanting immediately changes:
> "Eighteen... Eighteen... Eighteen..."

Challange:
Play rock paper scissor using Phishing, Password, MFA, Social Engineering, Training
Phishing beats Password
MFA beats Phishing
Social Engineering beats MFA
Training beats Social Engineering


You open the warehouse door.
Four goblins stare back at you.
For a moment, nobody moves.
Then, with four synchronized sighs, they slump their shoulders and sulk.


“Oh, no we need to gear up the protagonist” grumbles the smallest one.

The others reluctantly begin shuffling around the warehouse, gathering your gear for the up coming adventure. One lazily drags your boots across the floor. Another struggles beneath the weight of your pack, stopping every few steps to complain. A third pokes through your supplies, occasionally pocketing something before remembering what they were supposed to be doing.

The fourth goblin sits on your sword and refuses to move.
The goblin huffs.
Then he suddenly looks up, horrified.
“Wait.”
His face falls.
“We have to reset the count.”
A long silence fills the room.
Then all four goblins groan.
